Delete your {{APP_NAME}} account

Applies to the {{APP_NAME}} app by {{DEVELOPER}} (package {{PACKAGE}})

Delete from inside the app (instant)

  1. Open {{APP_NAME}} and go to Settings → Privacy & data.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm twice ("Continue", then "Delete forever").

Deletion runs immediately on our servers and cannot be undone.

Or request deletion by email

If you can no longer access the app, email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} from the address associated with your account with the subject "{{APP_NAME}} account deletion request". Requests are honored within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • All your content
  • Any files stored in the cloud on your behalf
  • Usage records and subscription entitlement state
  • The account itself (sign-in link or email credential)

What is kept, and for how long

  • Nothing is retained by {{APP_NAME}} after deletion completes.
  • Purchase/billing records are held by Google Play under Google's own policies — {{APP_NAME}} never stores your payment details.
  • Files on your own device remain until you uninstall the app or delete them yourself.
