Delete your Yap account

Applies to the Yap app by mehdiacho (package app.greenforrest.yap)

Delete from inside the app (instant)

  1. Open Yap and go to Settings → Privacy & data.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm twice ("Continue", then "Delete forever").

Deletion runs immediately on our servers and cannot be undone.

Or request deletion by email

If you can no longer access the app, email mehdiacho@gmail.com from the address associated with your account with the subject "Yap account deletion request". Requests are honored within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • All notes and transcripts
  • All voice recordings stored in the cloud
  • Usage records, referral records, and subscription entitlement state
  • The account itself (Google sign-in link or email credential)

What is kept, and for how long

  • Nothing is retained by Yap after deletion completes. There is no additional retention period on our side.
  • Purchase/billing records are held by Google Play under Google's own policies — Yap never stores your payment details.
  • Files on your own device (exports you saved, the app's local data) remain until you uninstall the app or delete them yourself.

Deleting data without deleting your account

You can also delete individual notes (Library → select → Delete; trashed notes purge after 30 days), clear downloaded audio (Settings → Privacy & data → Clear downloads), and choose an audio-retention policy (keep, discard after transcription, or auto-delete after 30 days). An export of all your data is available at Settings → Privacy & data → Export my data.
