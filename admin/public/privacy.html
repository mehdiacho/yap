Yap — Privacy Policy

Effective: 13 July 2026 · Applies to the Yap Android app (package app.greenforrest.yap)

Yap is a voice idea-capture app: you record a thought, Yap transcribes it and turns it into an organized note. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and the control you have over it. The short version: your recordings and notes are yours — we process them only to provide the features you use, we don't sell data, and we don't show ads.

Data we collect and why

  • Voice recordings (microphone). Created only when you start a recording. Stored on your device, and — unless you enable on-device-only mode — uploaded to your private cloud storage area so your notes sync across devices and can be transcribed.
  • Transcripts and notes. The text produced from your recordings, plus edits you make. Stored in your private cloud database area and on your device.
  • Account information. If you sign in: your email address, name, and profile photo from your Google account or the email address you register with. Guest use is supported — a random anonymous ID is used instead.
  • Usage metering. Minutes of cloud transcription used per month and storage bytes, kept to enforce plan quotas.
  • Purchase state. If you buy a subscription or unlock, Google Play processes the payment; we receive and store only a purchase token and the entitlement it grants — never your payment details.

How processing works

  • On-device first. Live draft transcription runs through your device's speech recognizer while you speak.
  • Cloud refinement. When a note is "polished", the audio or draft text is processed by our backend (Google Cloud Functions) which calls Google's Gemini API to transcribe and format it. Google processes this data as a service provider; it is not used to identify you.
  • Storage and sync. Notes and audio live in Google Firebase (Firestore and Cloud Storage) in a per-user area only your signed-in account can access, in the africa-south1 (Johannesburg) region. Data is encrypted in transit.
  • Bring-your-own-key. If you use your own AI provider key, it is stored only on your device and sent per-request; we never store it on our servers.

What we don't do

  • No selling of personal data. No advertising, and no third-party advertising SDKs.
  • No access to your contacts, location, photos, or messages.
  • No recording unless you start one — the microphone is used only during an active recording session you initiate, shown by a persistent notification.

Your controls

  • Export. Settings → Privacy & data → "Export my data" produces a zip of your notes and on-device audio.
  • Delete a note. Notes moved to trash are recoverable for 30 days, then permanently removed (device, database, and cloud audio).
  • Delete your account. Settings → Privacy & data → "Delete account" permanently removes your notes, recordings, usage records, and the account itself from our servers.
  • Audio retention. Settings let you keep audio, discard it after transcription, or auto-delete it after 30 days.
  • On-device only mode. Prevents audio from being sent to the cloud.

Retention

Your content is retained until you delete it or delete your account. Trashed notes are purged about 30 days after trashing. Backups of inactive free accounts may be deleted after six months of inactivity, with notice where possible.

Children

Yap is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
